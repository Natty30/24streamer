Broadcasting & Copyright Responsibility

Users are solely responsible for ensuring that they possess all requisite rights, licenses, releases, and permissions for any video or audio streamed via this application. The application does not provide, host, or bundle third-party copyrighted material.

1. Developer & Application Identity

Application: 24/7 Streamer (Continuous Broadcast)

Developer: 24/7 Streamer Team

Privacy Contact Email: appmalume@gmail.com

24/7 Streamer is designed to empower content creators and broadcasters to stream locally-stored pre-recorded videos to RTMP and RTMPS ingest destinations (such as YouTube Live, Facebook Live, Twitch, Kick, or custom RTMP streaming servers).

2. No User Accounts & No Personal Registration

24/7 Streamer does not require or provide user account creation, sign-up, or login. You do not need an email, username, or password with our application to utilize its full capabilities. We do not maintain any cloud-based user directory or account database.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

The application integrates the official Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to offer voluntary streaming time rewards and support ongoing development:

  • Rewarded Ads: Users may voluntarily tap "Watch Ad (+30m)" to watch a rewarded video advertisement. Upon verified completion callback received from the Google Mobile Ads SDK, +30 minutes of streaming balance is added locally to the device. Rewards are strictly tied to completed views and are never triggered involuntarily.
  • Banner & Interstitial Ads: The app displays a non-intrusive banner advertisement at the bottom edge. Interstitial ads may appear only during natural transitions (such as after a broadcast has ended). Interstitial ads are never displayed while an active live stream is running.
  • Data Processed by Google Mobile Ads: AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including Google Advertising ID), coarse location/IP address, and app interaction diagnostic telemetry for ad personalization, frequency capping, and fraud prevention in accordance with Google Play Advertising Policies.

4. Consent Management & Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

To adhere to applicable international privacy regulations (such as GDPR in the European Economic Area, UK GDPR, and US state privacy regulations), 24/7 Streamer incorporates the official Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK.

  • Upon application launch, applicable users are presented with clear privacy and consent choices.
  • Ad requests are only initiated when the user's consent state permits them.
  • Users can review or update their privacy and advertising preferences at any time by opening the in-app "Settings & Privacy Policy" dialog and tapping "Manage Privacy & Ad Consent Preferences".

5. Device Hardware, Storage & Permissions

24/7 Streamer strictly respects the principle of least privilege regarding device access:

  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The application broadcasts user-selected media files directly. It does not request, access, or utilize camera or microphone permissions or hardware sensors.
  • Local Media Selection: Users choose their own video files using the standard Android system document picker (ACTION_OPEN_DOCUMENT). This zero-permission mechanism avoids requesting broad storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_VIDEO). Video files remain in your local storage and are never uploaded to any developer server.
  • Notifications: The POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission is used solely to display an active Foreground Service status notification that reports streaming time remaining and provides an instant "STOP STREAM" control.
  • Network & Wake Lock: INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are used to connect and transmit RTMP packets to the user's chosen server. WAKE_LOCK keeps the CPU active during live broadcast sessions to prevent playback stutter.

6. Direct RTMP Ingestion & No Intermediary Servers

Video encoding and RTMP/RTMPS packetization occur strictly on your Android device. Audio and video frames are transmitted directly from your device to the ingest URL you provide (e.g. YouTube Live, Twitch, or Facebook Live).

The application developer does not run intermediate relay servers, video proxy nodes, or cloud recording servers. Your live stream content is never intercepted, stored, or viewed by the developer.

7. Stream Key & Credential Encryption

Stream keys and RTMP server URLs are sensitive broadcaster credentials. When you choose to save them, they are encrypted locally using the Android KeyStore system with hardware-backed AES-256 GCM encryption.

Credentials are never written to unencrypted log files, never included in crash diagnostics, and never transmitted to any third party.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

All user preferences, video URI references, streaming time balances, and encrypted keys reside 100% locally on your device.

  • In-App Deletion: You can permanently delete all stored credentials and preferences at any time by tapping "Reset All Saved Credentials & Data" in the Privacy Policy dialog.
  • System Settings: You can also clear all stored data through Android Settings → Apps → 24/7 Streamer → Storage → Clear Data.

9. Children's Privacy

24/7 Streamer is designed for general audiences and content broadcasters. It is not intended for or directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological, operational, or legal changes. Any modifications will be posted inside the application and made available at this public URL.
